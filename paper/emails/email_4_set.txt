Subject: Independent TLE-derived density signal for JB2008 / HASDM cross-validation

Dear SET team,

I'm an independent researcher in orbital dynamics. I recently developed and open-sourced thermoLEO, a method that extracts a thermospheric density signal in the 800-1000 km range using only public TLE data.

Key results:

- Secular collapse of altitude skewness in the 823-925 km band (2014-2025)
- Lower tail (q05) decayed -113 km while upper tail (q95) decayed only -17 km
- Correlation skewness x lower tail: r = -0.98; regression R2 = 0.986

Why this may matter to SET: the method could serve as an independent cross-validation channel for HASDM at 800-1000 km, where dedicated accelerometer data are limited.

What I propose: a technical exchange with your team. I'd be glad to share the code, data, and full analysis.

Attached: one-pager (PDF) and repository link.
