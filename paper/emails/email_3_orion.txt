Dear Mr. Adams,

I'm an independent researcher working on orbital dynamics. I recently open-sourced thermoLEO, a method to estimate thermospheric density in the 800-1000 km range from public TLE data.

Key results:

- Skewness collapse in the 823-925 km band from +0.12 to -1.85 over 2014-2025
- Lower-tail decay 6.6x faster than upper-tail (-113 km vs -17 km)
- R2 = 0.986 with F10.7 and quantile regression

Why this may matter to Orion: this could serve as an independent validation channel for the Dragster density model, or as a low-cost complementary source for customers outside the HASDM ecosystem.

What I propose: a technical call with your team to explore collaboration.
